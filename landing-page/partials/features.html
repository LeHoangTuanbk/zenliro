Features

Everything a photographer needs

RAW Processing

Import RAW, JPG, PNG, WebP, TIFF and more. View EXIF metadata and histogram at a glance.

Color Grading

Tone curve, HSL, color mixer, split toning. Every tool a photographer expects.

AI Agent

AI analyzes, plans, and edits your photo in real-time. Watch it work like a photographer at the controls.

Full undo/redo history. Your original file is never touched. Every edit is tracked and reversible.

Style Presets

40+ curated looks for different moods and genres. One-click to transform your photo's feel.

Custom shaders for real-time processing entirely on the GPU. Instant feedback on every adjustment.

Photo Library

Intuitive folder-based photo management with drag-and-drop support. Stay organized as your shoots grow.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Intuitive shortcuts modeled after Lightroom Classic. Keep your hands on the keyboard and your eyes on the photo.

Hand a batch of photos to the AI Agent. It edits each one and notifies you when the whole set is done.
